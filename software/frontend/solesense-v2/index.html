Run analysis
Cadence
-- spm
How often your feet hit the ground. Healthy distance running: 170-180 spm.
Ground contact
-- ms
How long each foot stays planted. Quicker is better; over 300 ms is long.
Impact rate
--
How quickly force builds when you land. High values predict stress fractures.
Pronation
-- deg/s
Positive: rolls inward. Negative: rolls outward. Some pronation absorbs impact.

Pressure split between medial (inside) and lateral (outside) edges of the foot.
